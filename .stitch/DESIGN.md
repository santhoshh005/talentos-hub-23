## Brand & Style

TalentOS is a modern, dark-mode-first recruitment CRM. The visual style relies heavily on **Glassmorphism**, vibrant neon-cyan/blue gradients, and deep slate backgrounds.

## Colors

- **Background**: Deep Slate (`#020617`)
- **Cards/Panels**: Lighter Slate (`#0F172A`) with glassmorphism blur and subtle white borders (8% opacity).
- **Primary**: Electric Blue (`#2563EB`)
- **Accent**: Neon Cyan (`#38BDF8`)

## Elevation & Depth

Cards and panels use a glassmorphism effect: semitransparent background, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, and a subtle 1px border. Shadows are soft and glowing, often tinted with the primary blue/cyan gradient.

## Shapes

Components use a generous corner radius (`14px` base) for a friendly, modern tech feel.
